# V44 · Heighliner

Heighliner is V44.3, named for the Guild ships of _Dune_ that fold space, because the gate got this release. On an ultrawide screen the ring had looked low in resolution and hard to read. It is now a hoop with real depth: a lit deck with running lights, the far side's inner wall washed by the gate's own light, the near side's outer wall in shadow with lit ports, and four clamps under the red beacons, with every edge one pixel sharp. The live gate now times a frame on the GPU now and then and, while frames are cheap, climbs a step at a time to the screen's own pixels, up to a native 4K screen; a paused gate sharpens itself the same way, and the painted posters are painted again with larger sizes for 4K and ultrawide screens. A film chosen in a room now plays in that room and closing it returns to the same spot; the preserved V43 edition returns a guest to the room they came from, says that it is an archive and points to the current site. The rooms carry the front door's mark and wordmark.

Bitmaxing was V44.2, named by Doug for Bit, the copilot in the corner, who got most of this release. Bit is a glass icosahedron in platinum around a lit core, with a ring and a small red moon that leaves a wake. It turns while the visitor is active and then rests, leans and looks toward the pointer, blinks now and then, thinks along while E.V.E. works out an answer (the ring wobbles, a second ring crosses it and the moon hurries), swells and throws sparks when tapped or pleased, and goes red and nearly still while a request is held for a person. Its card opens from its corner and shows the same model large. The Workshop system map and the Helm route map are redrawn as hull plates on a drafting grid with running marks, packet trails and a return mark; the Helm resolves its lane's name out of noise; the four worlds arrive one after another; the screen's play button sends out a ring of light while it waits. Pause and reduced motion leave every one of these still and complete. Prepared on 11-12-2026; rollback for this round is the previous production commit `d31f5cf453c547c0ec95c1af055d98146076ad5a`.
