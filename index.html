BUILD STORY / SOVEREIGN STARSHIP

One boundary. Every request accounted for.

A ship you can explore. An AI decision you can see. I built this working model to make the trade-offs between local computing, cloud access, and human permission tangible.

The question
What happens to private work when the cloud is available, disappears, or needs permission?
My design decision
Keep the workload fixed at twelve requests. Connect the ship, the counters and the explanation to the same rule. Let a visitor change the boundary and see the consequence.
The working result
In Hybrid, six private requests stay aboard and six public requests can use the cloud. Disconnect the relay and all twelve stay local. Choose cloud-only processing for private work without permission, and all twelve wait for human review.
