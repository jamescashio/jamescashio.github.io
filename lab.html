Architecture by function

Public documentation describes responsibilities and design decisions—not the private coordinates needed to reach the systems.

Compute Fabric

Virtualized workloads, local inference, workload isolation, and resilient capacity across multiple compute classes.

AI Gateway

Capability- and cost-aware routing across specialized model lanes with fallback paths and local-first options.

Automation Plane

Event-driven workflows, scheduled jobs, agent tools, verification steps, and human approval boundaries.

Security Plane

Authentication, least privilege, input validation, logging, segmentation, and fail-closed service behavior.

Storage Plane

Backups, retained artifacts, encrypted recovery paths, and separation between public presentation and private operations.

Observability

Health, cost, and reliability signals are collected privately; only coarse portfolio metrics are published.
