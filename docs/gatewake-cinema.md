# V37.4 Lensing: Gatewake — The gate awakens

Original cinematic artwork based on the existing Cashio orbital hero artwork. The six-second shot animates a quiet approach, gold core activation and a late teal aurora. It is an imagined environment, not operational footage or a physical simulation.

The web film is silent H.264 at 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, 145 frames (6.04 seconds), with faststart metadata. Its size is 1,645,726 bytes. The 1600 × 900 WebP poster is 87,202 bytes. The film's SHA-256 is `d3a834e3231c2e8c455e4b34e225844ed0e333ee39dc23ebd5c0bd7e6b00e1d6`; the poster's SHA-256 is `92f9249471bf095cf1e4fcfdc8a511c741741daa8ea84a9dc9f682ce729dddf7`.

The cinema is loaded only on request. Neither film autoplays or loops; video uses `preload="none"`, and the poster remains until explicit playback. Reduced-motion visitors can still choose Play. Hiding the page pauses playback. Selecting another film stops the departing player and resets its replacement to the poster.

`#film` preserves Orbital arrival; `#film=awakening` opens The gate awakens. The new film's Enter this world action is available before and after playback. It opens the interactive observatory at the gate in eclipse lighting with resonance enabled. Closing the observatory returns focus to the original cinema launcher. Entering through the usual observatory button retains Dawn, Orbit and unlit resonance.
