# Continuum

V37.10 connects the Sanctuary film to a visitor-operated three-dimensional chamber, refines the ship's material response, and gives the mobile opening a clear thirty-second starting journey. Existing film links, original assets and the dated public archive remain available.

Entry, camera movement, selection and light controls are explicit. Motion obeys pause, visibility and reduced-motion settings. The renderer and its styles are lazy, and a still image remains usable when WebGL is unavailable.
