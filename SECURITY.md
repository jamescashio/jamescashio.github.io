# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security issues affecting **cashio.us** or this public repository to **admin@cashio.us**.

Include:

- the affected page, file, or behavior;
- clear reproduction steps;
- the potential impact;
- any supporting screenshots or request/response details; and
- a safe way to contact you for follow-up.

Please do not publish exploit details, access non-public systems, retrieve data that is not your own, disrupt service, or perform denial-of-service testing.

## Scope

In scope:

- the public website at `https://cashio.us`;
- public GitHub Pages content in this repository; and
- accidental disclosure of credentials, private infrastructure details, or personal information in the public repository.

Out of scope:

- social engineering;
- physical testing;
- third-party services not operated by the site owner; and
- private systems or services that are not intentionally exposed to the public internet.

## Response goals

Reports will be acknowledged as promptly as practical. Valid findings will be prioritized according to impact, exposure, and exploitability.

## Public-repository rule

This repository is a portfolio and presentation layer. It must not contain production credentials, private addresses, internal deployment instructions, customer information, employer-confidential information, household information, or detailed operational telemetry.
