# Privacy Notice for cashio.us

**Last updated:** 10-15-2026

cashio.us is a public professional portfolio operated by Doug Cashio.

## Information intentionally published

The site may publish professional information such as:

- name and professional role;
- portfolio descriptions and technical skills;
- public professional-profile links;
- a domain-based contact address; and
- coarse, presentation-oriented lab metrics.

The site is not intended to publish home addresses, personal phone numbers, personal email accounts, family information, customer data, employer-confidential information, credentials, or detailed private infrastructure data.

## Visitor data

The site does not intentionally require accounts, forms, advertising identifiers, or behavioral profiling. Standard web-hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp, and diagnostic information.

Some portfolio pages may request fonts, styles, or other static assets from third-party content-delivery providers. Those providers can receive ordinary connection metadata, including the visitor's IP address and browser request details. The public-safe `lab.html` page is self-contained and does not load third-party scripts or fonts. External profile links lead to third-party services with their own privacy practices.

## Cookies and analytics

No first-party advertising cookies are intentionally set, and no first-party behavioral analytics are intentionally operated. If analytics are added later, this notice should be updated before deployment and the implementation should minimize collection, retention, and third-party sharing.

Cloudflare Web Analytics automatic script injection was disabled for cashio.us on 8 September 2026. Ordinary hosting and security request metadata remains subject to the hosting providers' practices.

## Optional YouTube film

The Sovereign uses YouTube's privacy enhanced player at `youtube-nocookie.com`. Its poster is served by cashio.us. The player loads only after you select Play. At that point Google and YouTube can receive connection and playback information, including your IP address, browser details and referring site, under their own [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

[Privacy enhanced mode](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780) limits how embedded views influence personalization; it does not mean the player is free of all data collection. The player starts muted. Closing it or leaving the film stops playback. The direct Watch on YouTube link opens YouTube with its own privacy practices.

## Interactive scenes and downloads

The current homepage and interactive rooms store your manual motion preference in this browser under `cashio-v40-motion`. The preserved Helios edition uses `cashio.helios.motion`. These preferences are not transmitted to the site operator. Your device's reduced-motion setting takes priority. If browser storage is unavailable, the motion control still works for the current visit.

The flight, mission outcomes, and public studies run in your browser with illustrative sample data. They do not send AI requests or connect to the private infrastructure described in the portfolio. Saving a mission card creates an image in your browser and downloads it to your device; the site does not upload that image. A shared scene link contains the selected demonstration settings. Observatory links also preserve lighting, atmosphere and camera framing in the fragment after `#`; the recipient opens a paused scene. No screenshot or scene image is uploaded when creating that link.

## Security and retention

Operational telemetry, detailed logs, deployment information, security journals, and private infrastructure records are kept outside the public presentation layer. Public telemetry is intentionally coarse and should not be treated as a real-time operational dashboard.

## Contact

Questions about this notice or accidental information disclosure may be sent to **admin@cashio.us**.
