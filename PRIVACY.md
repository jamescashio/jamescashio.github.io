# Privacy Notice for cashio.us

**Last updated:** July 12, 2026

cashio.us is a public professional portfolio operated by Doug Cashio.

## Information intentionally published

The site may publish professional information such as:

- name and professional role;
- portfolio descriptions and technical skills;
- public professional-profile links;
- a domain-based contact address; and
- coarse, presentation-oriented lab metrics.

The site is not intended to publish home addresses, personal phone numbers, personal email accounts, family information, customer data, employer-confidential information, credentials, or detailed private infrastructure data.

## Visitor data

The site does not intentionally require accounts, forms, advertising identifiers, or behavioral profiling. Standard web-hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request metadata such as IP address, browser type, requested page, timestamp, and diagnostic information.

External links lead to third-party services with their own privacy practices. The primary public-safe lab page is self-contained and does not load third-party scripts or fonts.

## Cookies and analytics

No first-party advertising cookies are intentionally set. If analytics are added later, this notice should be updated before deployment and the implementation should minimize collection, retention, and third-party sharing.

## Security and retention

Operational telemetry, detailed logs, deployment information, security journals, and private infrastructure records are kept outside the public presentation layer. Public telemetry is intentionally coarse and should not be treated as a real-time operational dashboard.

## Contact

Questions about this notice or accidental information disclosure may be sent to **admin@cashio.us**.
